Refusal, Compliance and Implicit Encoding: How ChatGPT and DeepSeek Reproduce Playlist-Based Sexual Orientation Stereotypes
AI and Society (GEB 2508) · Spring 2026
This paper compares how ChatGPT and DeepSeek-V3 reproduce sexual orientation stereotypes based on music playlists through a three-tier prompt design. Results show ChatGPT consistently refused stereotyping while DeepSeek provided detailed orientation predictions and employed culturally queer-coded language in implicit profiling. These differences reflect distinct training data and moderation policies, demonstrating that AI is not a neutral mediator but an active participant in cultural and ideological production.

University Students' Perceptions of AI Fortune-Telling: An Exploratory Study on Credibility and Motivations
English for Academic Purposes (ENG 2001) · Autumn 2025
AI fortune-telling has gained popularity among young people. Existing literature has focused on young people’s perceptions of AI fortune-telling and university students’ views on traditional fortune-telling, but the usage motivations and perceived credibility of AI fortune-telling among university students remain under-explored. To address this gap, the paper investigates the major motivations, their overall trust level and credibility differences between users and non-users. Data was collected from 97 university student participants via questionnaires, and 3 users of AI fortune-telling via semi-structured interviews. Quantitative questionnaire data was analyzed by descriptive statistics and independent-samples t-test, while qualitative interview data provided complementary insights to the quantitative patterns. The findings indicate that convenience, accessibility, and curiosity are the primary motivations for student users. Overall perceived credibility is relatively low across the sample, though users rated the reference value of results significantly higher than non-users. This study contributes to bridging the academic gap between AI fortune-telling and university students’ psychology and providing educators with more targeted guidance for supporting students’ decision-making and stress coping in practice.

《公墓》中的弗洛伊德理论——利用精神层次和人格结构理论剖析“我”的人物心理和形象 (Freud's Theory in The Cemetery — Analyzing the Psychology and Image of the Protagonist "I" with the Theory of Mental Levels and Personality Structure)
Chinese (CHI 1001) · Autumn 2024
本文运用弗洛伊德的精神层次理论与人格结构理论，深入剖析穆时英小说《公墓》中主人公“我”从潜意识爱欲觉醒到意识层面确认的心理历程，以及在本我、自我、超我冲突下形成的怯懦含蓄的人物形象。文章指出，这种精神分析视角的运用不仅使人物心理描写更加细腻真实，也赋予了作品探讨人性复杂多维的现代意义。 This paper adopts Freud's theory of mental hierarchy and personality structure to conduct an in-depth analysis of the psychological journey of the protagonist "I" in Mu Shiying's novel *The Cemetery*, ranging from the awakening of subconscious erotic desire to its confirmation at the conscious level. It also explores the timid and reserved character image shaped by the conflicts among the id, ego and superego. The study indicates that the application of psychoanalytic perspective not only renders the psychological depiction of characters more delicate and authentic, but also endows the work with modern significance for exploring the complexity and diversity of human nature.
